Needid is an interactive installation created to stimulate critical thinking about what makes us human.

The project

NeedID was born as a communication machine and speculative design artifact: it does not propose solutions, but aims to make people reflect on the theme of the superfluous that characterizes us as human beings.

Download report

Technologies

A Kinect sensor was used to operate the machine, programmed with Processing, which communicates with the Arduino that controls the motors. Finally, the gears were made in 3D printing.

Events

The installation was exhibited both during the end-of-course exhibition at BASE Milano and during the Fuorisalone. The project was also presented with a the commercial video.
